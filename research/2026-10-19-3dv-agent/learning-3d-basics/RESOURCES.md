# 3D Reconstruction Resources

## Knowledge

- [COLMAP Tutorial](https://colmap.github.io/tutorial.html)
  Official explanation of the classical Structure-from-Motion and Multi-View Stereo pipeline. Use for: correspondences, camera recovery, sparse reconstruction, and dense reconstruction.
- [OpenCV Camera Calibration and 3D Reconstruction](https://docs.opencv.org/3.4.7/d9/d0c/group__calib3d.html)
  Official reference for the pinhole camera model, intrinsics, extrinsics, projection, and calibration.
- [VGGT, CVPR 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Wang_VGGT_Visual_Geometry_Grounded_Transformer_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf)
  Primary source for modern feed-forward prediction of cameras, depth, point maps, and tracks.
- [ETH3D high-resolution multi-view benchmark](https://eth3d.ethz.ch/high_res_multi_view)
  Official definitions and results for accuracy, completeness, F-score, and runtime.
- [DTU multi-view stereo benchmark paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-016-0902-9)
  Primary benchmark source. Use for: controlled multi-view capture and the accuracy–completeness tradeoff.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [COLMAP GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/colmap/colmap/discussions)
  Practitioner discussions for diagnosing real SfM and MVS failures.
