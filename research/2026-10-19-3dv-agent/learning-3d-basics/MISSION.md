# Mission: Understand 3D reconstruction well enough to lead this paper

## Why
Build enough practical 3D vision intuition to decide what the proposed training-free reconstruction-recovery paper should claim, which experiments are necessary, and whether an agent adds real value.

## Success looks like
- Explain the reconstruction pipeline from images to cameras, depth, and fused geometry.
- Distinguish camera, depth, correspondence, and fusion failures from evidence.
- Read benchmark tables and choose a compact, defensible CVPR experiment.

## Constraints
- Start from first principles and define every 3D term.
- Prefer concrete visual examples over matrix derivations.
- Keep the paper scoped as a quick CVPR submission.

## Out of scope
- Implementing classical multi-view geometry from scratch.
- A full course on projective geometry or neural rendering.
